Caring for Wool Jackets

Heavy wool needs very little washing. With a brush, fresh air and a careful hand, a good wool jacket can last for decades.

6 minute read · Reviewed November 2026

I. Brush after wearing

A stiff clothes brush removes dust, mud and loose fibres before they settle into the weave. Brush downward, with the grain of the cloth, after each muddy day. It takes a minute and keeps the wool looking fresh.

II. Air instead of washing

Wool naturally resists odour.

  • Hang the jacket outside or by an open window for a few hours.
  • Use a wide, shaped hanger so the shoulders keep their form.
  • Steam from a hot shower helps relax creases.

III. Spot cleaning

Blot spills straight away with a damp cloth. For marks, use a little wool wash in cool water and dab rather than rub. Let the area dry flat. For a full clean, follow the care label; many heavy wool jackets are dry clean only.

IV. Storing for summer

Clean the jacket before storing it, because moths are drawn to food and sweat marks. Keep it in a breathable cotton garment bag with cedar blocks or lavender sachets, away from damp and direct sun.

V. Small repairs

Re-sew loose buttons with strong thread before they fall off. Small moth holes can be darned with matching wool yarn. A good tailor can reline a jacket or patch worn elbows, which can add many years of life.
